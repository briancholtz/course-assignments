Your answers stay on your device. These assessments send nothing anywhere. Nothing is scored remotely, and your instructor never sees how you answered any individual item — only what you choose to paste into Canvas.

Complete each assessment individually and honestly. The point is an accurate starting picture, not a good-looking score.
