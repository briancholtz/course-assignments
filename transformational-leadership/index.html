Transformational Leadership Inventory

Twenty-eight statements about how you lead — the vision you set, the example you provide, and how you treat the people around you.

Items 28 Scale 1–7 Dimensions 7 Time ~10 min

Before you begin

Read each statement and choose the number that best describes how much you agree it fits you. Be as candid as possible — there are no right or wrong answers.

If you have never held a formal leadership position, forecast how you would behave in one. Think about group projects, teams, clubs, jobs, or any setting where others have looked to you.

How to read this. The six transformational dimensions describe behaviors that raise what a group aspires to. Contingent reward is a transactional behavior — exchanging recognition for performance. Effective leaders typically show both.

There are no published cutoff scores for this instrument. Compare your dimensions to each other rather than to a benchmark: the useful question is which behaviors come easily to you and which you would have to work at.
