Before you begin

Read each statement and choose the number that best describes your belief or behavior. Be as candid as possible — there are no right or wrong answers, and the value of this exercise comes entirely from the honesty of your responses.

Answer for how you actually behave, not how you would like to behave or how you think you should answer. A low score is not a verdict on your character; it is a starting point for the semester.

Your individual ratings stay on your device. This page sends nothing anywhere and your instructor never sees how you rated each statement. You submit only your total score and your written reflection.

Cultural Diversity Awareness Questionnaire items
01

I am aware of my own biases and how they affect my thinking.

02

I can honestly assess my strengths and weaknesses in the area of diversity and try to improve myself.

03

I assume good intent and ask for clarification when I don’t understand what was said or implied.

04

I challenge others when they make racial/ethnic/sexually offensive comments or jokes.

05

I speak up if I witness another person being humiliated or discriminated against.

06

I do not participate in jokes that are derogatory to any individual group.

07

I don’t believe that my having a friend of color means that I’m culturally competent.

08

I understand why a lack of diversity in my social circle may be perceived as excluding others.

09

I realize that people of other cultures have a need to support one another and connect as a group.

10

I do not make assumptions about a person or individual group until I have verified the facts on my own.

11

I have multiple friends from a variety of ethnicities and abilities.

12

I connect easily with people who look different from me and am able to communicate easily with them.

13

I’m interested in the ideas and beliefs of people who don’t think and believe as I do, and I respect their opinions even when I disagree.

14

I work to make sure people who are different from me are heard and accepted.

15

I recognize and avoid language that reinforces stereotypes.

16

I know others’ stereotypes associated with my ethnicity.

17

I encourage people who are culturally different from myself to speak out on their issues and concerns, and I validate their issues and concerns.

18

I avoid assuming that others will have the same reaction as I do when discussing or viewing an issue.

19

I understand that I’m a product of my upbringing and believe there are valid beliefs other than my own.

20

I do not take physical characteristics into account when interacting with others or when making decisions about others’ competence or ability.

21

I recognize that others stereotype me, and I try to overcome their perceptions.

22

I include people who are culturally different from myself in team decision-making processes that impact them.

23

I actively seek opportunities to connect with people who are different from me and seek to build rapport with them.

24

I believe “color blindness” is counterproductive and devalues a person’s culture or history.

25

I avoid generalizing behaviors or attitudes of one individual in a group to others.

26

I actively convey that employees or students of varying backgrounds are as skilled and competent as others.

27

I do not try to justify acts of discrimination to make the victim feel better. I validate their assessment of what occurred.

28

I try to learn about and appreciate the richness of other cultures and honor their holidays and events.

29

I believe there are policies and practices in place that negatively impact people outside the majority culture.

30

I understand the definition of internalized racism and how it impacts people of color.

31

I recognize that race is a social construct, not a scientific fact.

32

I know and accept that people’s experiences and background impact how they interact with and trust me.

What the score means

This self-assessment is designed to measure your beliefs and behavior regarding cultural diversity and inclusion. A higher score on the assessment indicates that you are acutely aware of prejudice and bias, and that you are very aware of the impact of your behavior on others. Individuals who score high relate to others in ways that value diversity. A lower score on the assessment suggests that you are unaware of prejudice and bias, and that you are not fully aware of the impact of your biased behavior on others. Individuals who score low communicate with others in ways that do not value diversity.
