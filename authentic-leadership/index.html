What each dimension means

Self-Awareness

Knowing your own strengths, weaknesses and feelings, and seeking feedback about them.

Internalized Moral Perspective

Being guided by your own values and morals rather than by group pressure.

Balanced Processing

Seeking and listening to other views, including ones that disagree with yours, before deciding.

Relational Transparency

Showing others who you really are, including your feelings and your mistakes.

Interpreting a dimension score

  • A score around 4–5 is in the high range.
  • A score around 3 is in the moderate range.
  • A score around 1–2 is in the low range.

These are rough guides rather than exact cutoffs, so a score near a boundary should be read as near a boundary. The more useful comparison is between your own four dimensions.

Authentic leadership is usually described as having four parts: knowing yourself, being guided by your own values, genuinely weighing other people’s views, and letting others see who you really are. A lower score on one dimension is not a verdict — it points at the part of authentic leadership that would take the most deliberate work for you.
